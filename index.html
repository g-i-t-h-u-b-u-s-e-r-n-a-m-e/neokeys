Setting Up Neokeys

On Chromium-based Browsers

  • Use the link generator below to get a link that uses your favourite search engine as a backup
  • Copy the link you generated
  • Click the 3 dots in the top-right corner
  • Click "Settings"
  • Click "Search Engine"
  • Click "Manage search engines and site search"
  • On the Settings tab, click the "Add" button to the right of "Site search" (you might need to scroll down)
  • Enter whatever you want under Name and Shortcut (but use something that makes sense to you)
  • Paste the generated URL under "URL with %s in place of query"
  • Click the "Add" button
  • Click the 3 dots next to the entry you just created for neokeys
  • Click "Make default"

On Firefox-based Browsers

  • Use the link generator below to get a link that uses your favourite search engine as a backup
  • Copy the link you generated
  • Click the 3 lines in the top-right corner
  • Click "Settings"
  • Click "Search"
  • Click the "Add" button at the bottom of the page
  • Enter whatever you want under "Search engine name" (but use something that makes sense to you)
  • Paste the generated URL under "URL with %s in place of search term"
  • Click the "Add Engine" button
  • Under "Default Search Engine" at the top of the page, select the one with the name you put in
